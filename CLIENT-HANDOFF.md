# Garg Dental Clinic & Implant Centre — Client Handoff Summary

Welcome to your new production-ready web platform for **Garg Dental Clinic & Implant Centre** in Kapurthala, Punjab.

---

## 🎯 What Has Been Built

1. **Complete 11-Page Web Platform**:
   - **Homepage (`/`)**: 16 structured, cohesive sections featuring hero experience, trust strip, treatment previews, why choose us, doctor overview, gallery preview, patient reviews, before/after slider, FAQs, location details, and footer.
   - **About Clinic (`/about`)**: Clinic story container, 4 care philosophy principles, patient journey timeline (01 to 05), patient experience, curated gallery, and review integration.
   - **Specialized Treatments (`/treatments`, `/treatments/dental-implants`, `/treatments/root-canal-treatment`)**: Comprehensive educational treatment guides with conservative medical wording.
   - **Doctor Experience (`/doctors`, `/doctors/lead-specialist`)**: Professional profile pages, qualification & registration matrix, and clinical practice focus areas.
   - **Patient Feedback (`/reviews`)**: Public Google Review rating summary (4.5/5 from 120 reviews), source attributions, and category filter tabs.
   - **Facility Gallery (`/gallery`)**: Curated photo gallery with category filter tabs and interactive multi-photo lightbox modal.
   - **Clinical Case Studies (`/before-after`)**: Interactive outcome comparison sliders, patient privacy protection architecture, and consent disclaimers.
   - **Patient Contact & Location (`/contact`)**: 4 contact action cards, landmark directions (*Adjacent to Bank of Baroda*), map preview, and clinic information card.
   - **Appointment Request System (`/book-appointment`)**: Validated request form with treatment pre-selection, anti-spam protection, and honest status messaging (*"Appointment Request Received"*).
   - **Branded 404 Page**: Branded error handler for unmatched URLs.

2. **Technical & Local SEO Setup**:
   - Schema.org `Dentist` / `LocalBusiness` / `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD markup.
   - Dynamic meta titles, meta descriptions, Canonical URLs, and Open Graph social tags.
   - Generated `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`.

3. **Privacy & Security**:
   - Zero patient identity data sent to analytics.
   - Form honeypot protection against spam bots.
   - Strict zero fabrication compliance across medical content.
